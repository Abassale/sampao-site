Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. SAMPAO does not require a player account.

Where is my progress?

The current iOS version stores progress and records locally on the device, using Capacitor Preferences. There is no cloud save. Removing the app may delete local progress.

Are ads or Premium active?

Ads are disabled in this TestFlight build. Premium purchases are not commercially enabled. All levels are free to play.

How can I report a crash?

Send feedback using Apple's TestFlight app or the support email above. Include device model, iOS version and how to reproduce the issue.

How do I remove my data?

Removing SAMPAO normally deletes local progress (subject to Apple iOS backups). Contact support with questions.
