Frequently asked questions

What is free?

The iPhone 1.0.7 version includes World 1 and selected modes for free. The non-consumable Full Game purchase unlocks the other worlds, challenges and modes. There is no subscription.

I already bought the full game. How do I restore it?

Go to Options → Purchases → RESTORE PURCHASES, or use the restore button on the paywall. Sign in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase. If it fails, email support without sharing payment information.

Where is my progress saved?

Your progress is stored on-device. Options → Save data → EXPORT allows you to choose where to keep a copy. Restoring a purchase does not restore game progress.

Who manages refunds?

App Store payments are managed by Apple. Visit Report a Problem for billing and refund requests.

How can I report a bug?

Email the issue and, if helpful, a screenshot without personal or payment details.
