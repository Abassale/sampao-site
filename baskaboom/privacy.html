Privacy Policy — BASKABOOM

iPhone application · Version 1.0.7

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Publisher and contact

Publisher: Alexandre Bassale, independent game developer. Privacy inquiries: alexandre.bassale56@gmail.com.

Data handled by the game

The iPhone 1.0.7 version does not require a player account or a continuous internet connection. It contains no advertisements, in-app audience analytics or ad tracking. The developer does not operate a server collecting player progress.

Local progress and backups

Completed levels, scores, preferences, daily challenges and replays are kept on the iPhone. The Export function generates a file and lets you choose its destination (Files, AirDrop, email, etc.). We do not receive this file unless you choose to send it to us.

In-app purchases

The non-consumable Full Game purchase and its restoration use Apple StoreKit and the App Store. Apple handles transactions and payment information. We do not receive your bank details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Diagnostics and support

Depending on iPhone settings, Apple may share diagnostics and crash information with the developer. If you contact support, we process the email address and information you send solely to respond and resolve the issue; communications are retained as reasonably necessary for support and legal obligations.

Children and rights

There is no chat or player registration. You may request access, correction or deletion of information held in support correspondence by emailing us. On-device progress is under your control, subject to iOS backup behavior.

This website

This static website has no advertising cookies or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages may process technical access information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We will update this policy if app features or data practices change.
