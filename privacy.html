Privacy Policy

How SAMPAO handles game progress, support questions and iOS services.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Summary: SAMPAO does not require an account. Game progress remains on the device. The current TestFlight version has ads disabled and no live Premium purchase.

Controller and contact

SAMPAO is an independent game. Privacy questions, requests and concerns may be sent to alexandre.bassale56@gmail.com.

Local data

The game stores progress, records, preferences and completed levels locally using iOS app storage and Capacitor Preferences. This version does not upload those saves to a SAMPAO-operated cloud service.

Apple services and feedback

Apple may process device, download, crash and diagnostics data via iOS, TestFlight and the App Store under Apple's privacy policies. We may receive diagnostic reports and feedback via those services. If you email us, we receive the email address and information you choose to include solely to provide support.

Advertising and in-app purchases

AdMob and StoreKit integration code exists, but advertising display is disabled and Premium is not commercially enabled in this version. The game does not currently request App Tracking Transparency permission. Third-party SDKs and services may handle technical data; App Store privacy disclosures should be verified against actual SDK behavior. We will review this notice before enabling ads or purchases. See Google privacy and Apple privacy.

Purposes and legal bases

Local state allows the game to work. Support correspondence is used to respond to requests and investigate problems. Where applicable under the GDPR, processing may rely on providing the requested service, legitimate interests or consent as required. We do not sell personal data.

Retention, deletion and rights

Local saves remain until removed from the device, subject to iOS backup settings. Support emails are kept only as reasonably necessary to respond and comply with applicable obligations. Contact us to request access, rectification or deletion of data we control, or to object where the law allows. For Apple-controlled data, consult Apple privacy settings.

Children and website

There is no in-game chat or social feed. Do not submit sensitive information in support messages. This static website has no analytics scripts, third-party fonts or intentional tracking cookies. GitHub Pages, if used, may process web access logs according to GitHub's privacy statement.

Updates

This notice may change when SAMPAO or its SDKs change. The date above will be updated.
