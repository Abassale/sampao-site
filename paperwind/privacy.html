Publisher and contact

How the game works

PAPERWIND is a flying and exploration game. The iPhone 3.3.2 edition provides a non-consumable Full Game purchase without subscriptions or in-game advertising. You do not need a support account to contact us.

Data and saves

Settings and game data may be kept locally on the device so that the game works. Please keep backups before uninstalling. The app does not request bank details, and the developer does not directly collect payment details.

Apple purchases

Purchases and restoration are processed through Apple services and may involve transaction data handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Diagnostics and support

Apple may provide diagnostics and crash information based on iOS sharing settings. If you email us, we process your email address and the details you provide to respond and investigate; we retain correspondence as long as reasonably needed for support and applicable obligations.

Your rights and younger players

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal data held in support correspondence by contacting us. Please do not send sensitive information. Certain diagnostics and backups are controlled by iOS settings.

Website hosting
