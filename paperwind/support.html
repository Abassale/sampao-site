Frequently asked questions

How do I unlock the full game?

Choose Full Game in the app. The in-app purchase is processed and billed by the App Store. Apple shows the current local price before confirmation.

I already bought the full game. How do I restore it?

Use the Restore Purchases option provided in the app. Sign in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase. If it fails, email support without sharing payment information.

Will reinstalling affect my progress?

Before uninstalling, make sure you have a compatible backup. Contact support if you need help identifying save options in the installed version.

Who manages refunds?

App Store payments are managed by Apple. Visit Report a Problem for billing and refund requests.

How can I report a bug?

Email the issue and, if helpful, a screenshot without personal or payment details.
